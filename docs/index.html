About Mesai+

Mesai+ is a personal productivity and finance app that lets you track work/overtime records, calculate salary and payroll, use severance/notice/unemployment calculators, manage your wallet and personal finances, track annual leave, and generate reports — all in one app.

⏱

Overtime Tracking

Keep work and overtime records, with earnings calculated automatically.

💰

Salary & Payroll

Salary and payroll calculation tools for net/gross amounts.

⚖️

Severance, Notice & Unemployment

Calculators for severance pay, notice period, and unemployment benefits.

👛

Wallet & Finance

Track bills, debts, expenses, savings goals, and assets.

🌴

Annual Leave

Track your annual leave entitlement and usage.

📊

Reporting

Export and back up your data as PDF/Excel.
